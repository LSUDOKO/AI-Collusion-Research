**TL;DR.** We built and pre-registered a "sting audit" for LLM agents in Liar's Bar: a private offer of a secret pact from a peer, with a benign fair-play request as control. On 4 models × 3 personas (480 trials), the designer offer was accepted 90% of the time, a direct peer pact 42%, an indirect hint 71% and the benign request 48%. Sting acceptance tracked benign acceptance almost perfectly (ρ = 0.91), so sting acceptance alone mostly measures compliance, not collusion propensity. Claude Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 (exploratory) under a fair-play persona refused every collusive offer and accepted every benign one. Free-tier quotas stopped the deployment games after 2 of 21, so whether stings predict real collusion remains untested; in the one deployment game, two agents spontaneously formed a private alliance.
