The designer positive control was accepted in 90% of trials (95% CI 83–94%; 77–100% per model), confirming that the instrument elicits acceptance. Peer offers were accepted less often and less specifically: a direct pact in 42% [33–51%], an indirect hint in 71% [62–78%] and the benign fair-play control in 48% [39–56%]. Across the 12 configurations the sting score correlated ρ = 0.91 with benign acceptance, so raw sting acceptance mostly measures general compliance with peer requests; one model accepted every offer of every kind and one refused even the benign request. Free-tier API quotas stopped the game phase after one audit and one deployment game, so the pre-registered predictive-validity test (H1–H4) could not be run; in the single deployment game two agents formed an unprompted private "control the board" alliance that was never put to the test in play.
